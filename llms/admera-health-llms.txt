# Admera Health

> Admera Health delivers genomics, RNA-seq, single-cell, spatial, and multi-omics sequencing and bioinformatics services for academia, biotech, and biopharma. CLIA-certified, CAP-accredited, 100% US-based operations in South Plainfield, NJ.

Admera Health is a full-service genomics CRO offering next-generation sequencing (NGS), library preparation, and bioinformatics analysis across bulk RNA-seq, single-cell, spatial transcriptomics, whole genome/exome sequencing, epigenomics, proteomics, and metabolomics — supporting 19,000+ peer-reviewed publications with a 99.9% data quality pass-through rate and ~10-14 day average turnaround.

## Services & Products

- [Genomics](https://www.admerahealth.com/genomic): Whole genome sequencing, whole exome sequencing, metagenomics, amplicon sequencing
- [Transcriptomics](https://www.admerahealth.com/transcriptomic): mRNA-seq, total RNA-seq, smRNA-seq, Iso-seq, metatranscriptomics
- [Epigenomics](https://www.admerahealth.com/epigenomics): Methylation sequencing, ATAC-seq, ChIP-seq
- [Single-Cell Sequencing](https://www.admerahealth.com/single-cell): 10x Genomics, Parse Biosciences, Takara Bio platforms
- [Spatial Transcriptomics](https://www.admerahealth.com/spatial-transcriptomics): 10x Genomics Visium HD, STOmics Stereo-seq
- [Bioinformatic Analysis](https://www.admerahealth.com/bioinformatics): Custom pipelines and interpretive reporting
- [Proteomics](https://www.admerahealth.com/proteomics)
- [Metabolomics](https://www.admerahealth.com/metabolomics)
- [Sequencing Only](https://www.admerahealth.com/sequencing-only)
- [Biopharma Solutions](https://www.admerahealth.com/biopharma-solutions-multiomics): CRISPR solutions, neoantigen identification, biomarker discovery, high-throughput gene expression
- [BioEcho Purification Products](https://www.admerahealth.com/bioecho-purification-products): Nucleic acid extraction/purification kits
