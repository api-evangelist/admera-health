Admera is certified as the first global service provider for Takara Bio’s Shasta® high-throughput, full-gene-body single-cell RNA-seq workflow, accelerating complex single-cell research.‍ Admera Health’s new CSP designation for the Shasta total RNA-seq workflow means researchers can leverage the platform's advanced capabilities for unprecedented detail and scale.
